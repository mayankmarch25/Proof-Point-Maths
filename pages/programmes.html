How placement actually works

School grade tells us which syllabus to cover. The diagnostic tells us where to start. A Class 10 student with an unclosed Class 7 algebra gap runs a bridging plan alongside the board syllabus, in the same batch, without being singled out.

One session a week, and why that works

Every batch meets once on a weekend, taught by the founder, with no substitute faculty. Students leave with a worked set and an error log. The session is where the method is taught and tested. The week in between is where it is practised.
